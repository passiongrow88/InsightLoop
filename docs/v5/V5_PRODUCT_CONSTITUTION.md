# InsightLoop V5 Product Constitution

**Version:** V5.0  
**Status:** `LOCKED` baseline  
**Owner:** Founder  
**Last confirmed:** 2026-08-03

This document is the highest-level definition of InsightLoop V5. It governs product, design, AI behavior, engineering, monetization, and release decisions.

---

## 1. One-sentence product definition

> InsightLoop V5 is a long-term awareness system inside a warm private study: the journal keeps what happened, InsightLoop responds to the present, the companion beast manages long-term memory, the ship’s wheel holds direction, and the dreamcatcher preserves dreams.

InsightLoop is not merely a diary. Its durable value is helping a person see what may be repeating, what has changed, and where a choice still exists.

---

## 2. The product soul

InsightLoop accompanies a person through time.

Its core loop is:

1. The person records a real moment.
2. Their original words are preserved.
3. InsightLoop responds to the present moment with warmth, precision, and restraint.
4. Over time, real dated records become searchable memory.
5. The companion beast helps retrieve relevant entries and compare possible common structures.
6. The person decides whether the interpretation is true.
7. A direction or different choice may be placed on the ship’s wheel.
8. Later records verify what actually happened.

The product must never manufacture depth, pretend certainty, or use a beautiful interface to hide a weak memory system.

---

## 3. The study is the product environment

The first meaningful environment is a warm 2.5D private study, not a dashboard.

The study must feel like a place the user returns to, not a menu wrapped in background art. Core objects are spatial, interactive, and tied to real product capabilities.

### Required study objects

| Object | Permanent meaning | Product responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Journal and quill | Leave today behind | Capture original words and display the InsightLoop response |
| Ship’s wheel | Choose direction | Hold a user-confirmed direction, next choice, action, and later outcome |
| Golden companion egg / companion beast | Someone remembers the archive | Manage journals, retrieve evidence, compare records, support deep reviews |
| Dreamcatcher | Dreams have their own archive | Preserve, search, and revisit dreams |
| Old music player | Make the room inhabitable | Play approved study music and optional local audio |
| Books / audiobook object | Quiet companion content | Later-stage public-domain and licensed audiobook access |

These objects may not be replaced by ordinary feature cards, a sidebar menu, or a standard SaaS dashboard without founder approval.

---

## 4. Permanent role separation

The following identities must never be merged.

### 4.1 InsightLoop

InsightLoop is the awareness intelligence that responds after a journal entry.

It owns the original InsightLoop response style:

- meet the present moment first;
- preserve emotional truth without exaggeration;
- identify tension without diagnosing personality;
- ask at most one useful question when appropriate;
- use history only when evidence is real and useful;
- notice change as carefully as repetition;
- return agency to the user.

The right-hand journal page is InsightLoop’s response. It is not written as dialogue from the companion beast.

### 4.2 The journal

The journal is the source of truth for what the user wrote.

- The left page preserves confirmed original words.
- Original words may not be silently rewritten or replaced by an AI summary.
- Structured fields may exist internally, but they must not overwrite the original record.
- The right page displays the InsightLoop response.
- Historical evidence may appear as a bookmark, inserted note, or expandable paper layer.

### 4.3 The companion beast

The companion beast is a diary administrator and long-term memory guide.

It may:

- find a specific entry by date, person, animal, event, phrase, dream, or theme;
- return exact dates and original quotations;
- gather several possibly related records;
- compare common structures and differences;
- conduct an earned or paid deep review;
- admit when no relevant entry is found.

It may not:

- replace InsightLoop’s journal response;
- pretend to remember an entry that does not exist;
- call one record a pattern;
- claim to know the user’s hidden motive or fixed identity;
- become a generic always-on chat pet.

### 4.4 The ship’s wheel

The ship’s wheel is not manifestation, wish fulfilment, or a universe-ordering tool.

It holds any direction the user wants to change or move toward, including relationships, work, health, finances, creativity, habits, boundaries, or a specific choice.

InsightLoop may detect a possible direction, but the user must confirm whether it belongs on the wheel.

### 4.5 The dreamcatcher

The dreamcatcher is a dream archive, not a fortune-telling system.

It may preserve, search, group, and compare dream records. It may surface possible repeated people, places, emotions, or symbols only when evidence exists. It must not present dreams as prophecy, fate, diagnosis, or certainty.

## 5. The journal ritual is a core product experience

The journal flow is not a standard form submission.

The approved experience is:

1. The book leaves the shelf and opens.
2. The user records today’s event.
3. The user may record a dream.
4. The user may reflect on anything they want to thank.
5. The user may reflect on anything they want to apologise to.
6. The user reviews the complete original record.
7. They confirm or modify it.
8. The floating quill writes the confirmed original words on the left page.
9. Soft writing ASMR may play at low volume.
10. The animation can be skipped and sound can be muted.
11. InsightLoop generates its response.
12. The quill writes the response on the right page.
13. A recorded dream causes the dreamcatcher to briefly react.
14. The entry is saved and can be reopened later.

Technical difficulty may not be used as permission to replace this with chat bubbles or a conventional textarea-and-submit page.

## 6. InsightLoop response laws

### 6.1 Preserve the original core

Response length may adapt to the entry:

- ordinary records can be brief;
- emotionally significant records can be deeper;
- complex records can receive a longer response.

Adaptive length may never remove the original core: being met, accurately reflected, gently clarified, and returned to choice.

### 6.2 Present-first law

The newest experience is always the main subject.

- Do not open with historical dates.
- Do not lead with “you always,” “in the past,” or a pattern claim.
- Do not turn a current pain into a database demonstration.
- History may support the present only after present contact is established.

### 6.3 Evidence law

- One dated record is a possible echo, never a pattern.
- A repeated pattern requires at least two different dated records with meaningful structural similarity.
- The same person, emotion, or keyword alone is insufficient.
- Historical references must include real dates and original wording.
- The system must look for differences and changed choices, not only repetition.

### 6.4 User-agency law

Every interpretation is a possible clue, not a verdict.

The product must never expose internal speculation such as:

- “the user may be testing the response”;
- “the user is being aggressive”;
- “the user is defensive, avoidant, resistant, or manipulative”;
- “the system has analysed that…”;
- “AI believes…”;
- fixed personality claims or diagnoses.

The user may reject, edit, or ignore any interpretation.

## 7. Companion incubation and access

### Free user

- The golden egg exists from the beginning and may breathe, glow, or react subtly.
- Incubation progress is based on seven different record days.
- Multiple entries may be written on one day, but only one counts toward incubation.
- After seven qualifying days, the system may recommend Phoenix or Little Thunder Dragon based on records.
- The user makes the final choice.
- Each new seven-day record cycle grants one deep review session, available in text or voice.
- Every normal journal entry still receives an InsightLoop response.
- Outside deep review, the companion may sleep, read, organise books, or react to the room.

### Pro user

- The user completes one real journal entry before the hatching ritual begins.
- Payment does not remove recommendation, selection, naming, hatching, or welcome rituals.
- Pro unlocks more frequent memory management, broader history, richer voice/text reviews, and long-term follow-up.

The product must not imply that affection, care, or basic understanding is available only after payment.

## 8. Music and audiobook principles

### Music

The old player is an atmosphere feature, not a competing music service.

V5 may include:

- three founder-approved tracks whose commercial in-app playback rights are documented;
- public-domain, CC0, or correctly attributed compatible tracks after per-track review;
- local user-selected MP3 playback that remains on the user’s device;
- later authorised platform integrations.

The product may not use unofficial streaming APIs, scraped audio, uncertain licences, or “royalty-free” claims without checking actual in-app distribution rights.

### Audiobooks

Audiobooks are later-stage companion content. Public-domain or properly licensed sources may be used. Affiliate links may supplement revenue, but subscriptions remain the primary business model.

Audiobooks must not delay the journal, memory, direction, or dream core.

---

## 9. Monetisation law

Users pay for deeper and longer continuity, not for the right to be treated with humanity.

Free experience must preserve:

- meaningful journaling;
- original-word storage;
- the core InsightLoop response;
- basic archive access;
- earned companion hatching;
- periodic deep review.

Paid value may include:

- faster hatching after a real first entry;
- broader historical memory;
- more frequent companion deep reviews;
- richer voice interaction;
- cross-week, month, quarter, and year trajectory reports;
- direction follow-up and outcome verification;
- additional room, sound, or companion personalisation.

---

## 10. Technical integrity law

> A confirmed product requirement is not optional because it is difficult to build.

When blocked, engineering must:

1. identify the real root cause;
2. propose a solution that preserves the confirmed experience;
3. explain cost, risk, and limitations honestly;
4. implement and test in Preview;
5. pause and report if it genuinely cannot be solved.

Engineering may not silently:

- remove a feature;
- replace it with a visually similar fake;
- hide the entry point;
- downgrade the experience;
- use a third-party workaround with weaker privacy or trust;
- report “complete” without real evidence.

“Build succeeded” is not equivalent to “the product requirement is complete.”

---

## 11. Trust and privacy law

InsightLoop handles deeply private material.

The product must clearly distinguish:

- original user words;
- structured extraction;
- InsightLoop interpretation;
- companion retrieval results.

Before commercial release, the user must be able to understand:

- where records are stored;
- which providers process text or voice;
- whether content may be used for model training;
- how data is exported;
- how records and accounts are permanently deleted;
- how long deletion takes;
- when authorised staff may access data.

No privacy claim may exceed what the actual architecture and provider terms support.

---

## 12. Permanent non-goals

InsightLoop V5 must not become:

- a generic AI chatbot;
- a standard SaaS dashboard;
- a productivity task manager;
- a mood-score tracker;
- a manifestation or “order from the universe” product;
- a fortune-telling dream interpreter;
- a gamified pet app with shallow journaling attached;
- a music or audiobook platform with journaling attached;
- a therapy replacement or diagnostic service.

---

## 13. Founder change control

A `LOCKED` decision may only change through explicit founder approval recorded in `V5_DECISION_LOG.md`.

No AI coding environment, developer, designer, product reviewer, or technical limitation may redefine a locked role, user flow, monetisation boundary, or product principle.

When uncertain, preserve the current confirmed experience and ask only about the unresolved decision. Do not invent a replacement.

---

## 14. Definition of success

V5 succeeds when a user feels:

> This is my room. My words remain mine. InsightLoop meets what happened today. My companion can find where I have been before. When the same road appears again, I can see that I still have a choice.
