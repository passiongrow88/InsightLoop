# InsightLoop V5 Decision Log

**Purpose:** Record founder decisions that coding agents and developers may not reinterpret.  
**Default status:** `LOCKED` unless explicitly marked otherwise.  
**Change authority:** Founder approval only.

---

## Status meanings

- `LOCKED` — confirmed decision; must be implemented as written.
- `OPEN` — unresolved decision; implementation must not invent an answer.
- `SUPERSEDED` — replaced by a later recorded decision.
- `EXPERIMENT` — temporary test with a defined success/failure condition.

---

## Locked decisions

### V5-001 — Product environment

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

InsightLoop V5 opens into a warm fixed-angle 2.5D private study with depth, local animation, and meaningful room objects.

It must not default to a SaaS dashboard, chat page, form page, or free-roaming 3D room.

---

### V5-002 — First-open order

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

An unauthenticated visitor sees the study first. They may open the journal and create the first record before registration. Account creation is requested when permanent save is required. The draft must survive registration and return.

---

### V5-003 — Core room objects

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The study contains:

- journal and quill;
- ship’s wheel wall object;
- golden companion egg;
- dreamcatcher;
- old music player;
- later-stage audiobook/books object.

Each object has a real product responsibility and may not be reduced to decoration.

---

### V5-004 — Journal fields

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The primary V5 journal flow keeps:

- today’s event;
- dream, optional;
- anything the user wants to thank, optional;
- anything the user wants to apologise to, optional.

Thanks and apology are not limited to people. The prompt must leave the object open for the user to decide.

---

### V5-005 — Single primary recording entry

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The study journal is the one primary record-entry experience. Legacy structured fields may remain internally or in an edit/review layer but may not compete as a second main journal module.

---

### V5-006 — Original words remain original

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The user reviews and confirms their complete original record. AI extraction or rewriting may not silently replace it.

---

### V5-007 — Journal presentation

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

After confirmation:

- the floating quill writes the user’s original words on the left page;
- writing ASMR may play softly;
- the user can mute and skip the animation;
- InsightLoop’s response is written on the right page;
- evidence may appear as a bookmark or inserted paper.

---

### V5-008 — InsightLoop response identity

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The right-page response belongs to InsightLoop, not the companion beast.

The response preserves the core of the original InsightLoop style. Its length adapts to the record, but its essential ability to meet the user, reflect accurately, remain restrained, and return agency must not be lost.

---

### V5-009 — Companion role

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

Phoenix and Little Thunder Dragon are diary administrators and memory guides.

They find real past entries, retrieve dates and original words, compare possible common structures and differences, and support deep reviews.

They are not the standard journal-response voice and must not become generic always-on chat pets.

---

### V5-010 — Present-first response

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

InsightLoop meets the newest moment first. It does not open with archive review, pattern claims, or dates. History serves the present and does not replace emotional contact.

---

### V5-011 — Pattern evidence threshold

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

- One dated related record is a possible echo only.
- A pattern needs at least two different dated records with structural similarity.
- Same person, feeling, or keyword alone is insufficient.
- Evidence uses real dates and validated original wording.
- The system must look for what changed as carefully as what repeated.

---

### V5-012 — Forbidden internal-analysis exposure

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

User-facing responses may not expose internal speculation such as:

- the user may be testing the response;
- aggressive/defensive/avoidant/resistant behaviour;
- hidden motives inferred from short or rude wording;
- “AI thinks” or “the system has analysed.”

Interpretations remain optional clues, never verdicts.

---

### V5-013 — Free companion incubation

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

A free user hatches a companion after seven qualifying record days.

- Days do not need to be consecutive.
- Multiple entries are allowed in one day.
- No more than one day credit is earned per calendar day.

---

### V5-014 — Companion recommendation and final choice

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The system recommends Phoenix or Little Thunder Dragon based on supported record signals. The user always makes the final choice and names the companion.

---

### V5-015 — Free deep-review access

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

After hatching, each new completed seven-day record cycle grants one companion deep-review session. The session may use text or voice.

Every normal journal entry still receives an InsightLoop response. The earned deep review is a separate entitlement.

---

### V5-016 — Pro hatching

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

A Pro user becomes eligible for the hatching ritual after completing one real saved journal entry.

Payment does not skip recommendation, choice, naming, hatching, or welcome.

---

### V5-017 — Companion room behaviour

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The companion uses several fixed room positions and state-linked behaviours rather than free roaming in the first release.

Examples include sleeping, reading, organising books, searching shelves, approaching the journal, or reacting to the dreamcatcher.

Unavailable deep interaction is represented naturally, not mainly through a harsh lock.

---

### V5-018 — Ship’s wheel meaning

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The ship’s wheel represents any direction or choice the user wants to change or move toward. It is available to free and paid users.

AI may detect a possible direction, but the user must confirm before it becomes active.

---

### V5-019 — No manifestation-order language

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

V5 does not use “order from the universe,” guaranteed attraction, or manifestation-order language. The wheel tracks direction, action, and actual outcome.

---

### V5-020 — Dreamcatcher meaning

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

When a dream is saved, the dreamcatcher briefly lights. It opens a dream archive for chronological browsing and search.

Repeated dream elements require real evidence. Dream interpretation may not become prophecy, fate, diagnosis, or supernatural certainty.

---

### V5-021 — Old music player

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The player is a study-atmosphere feature, not a full music platform.

The first release may include:

- three founder-selected tracks with documented commercial in-app playback rights;
- approved public-domain/CC0/compatible attributed tracks;
- local-device MP3 playback without server upload.

---

### V5-022 — Founder track source

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The founder’s three initial tracks are AI-generated. They may only be placed in the player after the generation platform and plan are verified to allow commercial full-track in-app playback to application users.

“Commercial use” alone is not assumed sufficient without checking the actual licence scope.

---

### V5-023 — External music services

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

Apple Music, Spotify, NetEase Cloud Music, Qishui Music, or other services may only be used through authorised commercial integrations and user permission.

Unofficial extraction APIs, scraped streams, or uncertain sources are prohibited.

---

### V5-024 — Audiobook priority

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

Audiobooks are later-stage companion content. Public-domain or properly licensed APIs and affiliate links may be explored, but they must not delay or dilute the journal, InsightLoop response, companion memory, ship’s wheel, or dreamcatcher.

---

### V5-025 — Free and paid emotional boundary

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

Free users receive the meaningful core InsightLoop response. Payment buys deeper and longer continuity, frequency, voice, reports, and personalisation—not basic warmth or the right to be understood.

---

### V5-026 — Technical non-bypass rule

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

When a confirmed experience causes a technical problem, the team must find and implement a requirement-preserving solution.

It may not silently delete, hide, imitate, replace, downgrade, or route around the requirement.

If the requirement cannot currently be achieved, work pauses and the blocker is reported honestly.

---

### V5-027 — Production protection

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

V5 work remains on independent Preview branches. `main`, `insightloop.lol`, production authentication, production data, and paid-user access may not be changed without explicit founder approval.

---

### V5-028 — Build success is not completion

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

A successful build or HTTP 200 response does not prove completion. Real user paths, persistence, provider calls, device behaviour, deployment identity, and acceptance tests must be verified.

---

### V5-029 — Privacy claims must be factual

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The product may only claim storage, encryption, no-training treatment, deletion timing, and provider behaviour that are supported by actual architecture and verified provider terms.

---

### V5-030 — One product soul

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `LOCKED`

The study, journal, InsightLoop, companion, wheel, dreamcatcher, player, and later audiobooks all serve one product soul: preserve today, retrieve the past, notice repetition and change, choose direction, and verify what follows.

No secondary feature may become the main product.

- plan/tier used when generated;
- generation date;
- track ownership terms;

### V5-OPEN-002 — Final Pro prices and usage limits

**Status:** `OPEN`

The free/Pro product boundary is locked, but exact price, currency presentation, fair-use limits, founding-member offer, and trial mechanics remain to be finalised.

### V5-OPEN-003 — Production model/provider set

**Status:** `OPEN`

MiMo may be used for development testing, but the final production provider mix, data terms, fallback provider, and cost controls require explicit validation.

### V5-OPEN-004 — Data deletion retention period

**Status:** `OPEN`

The user deletion promise must wait for confirmed database, backup, log, provider, and legal retention behaviour.

**Status:** `OPEN`

Imported old journals can accelerate meaningful memory, but whether they count toward the seven-day emotional hatching ritual remains undecided. Default implementation: they do not count.
